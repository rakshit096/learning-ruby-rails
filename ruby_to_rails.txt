What You Learned in Ruby,How Rails Uses It
Classes & OOP (class Student)-->Every controller (ApplicationController) and model (User < ApplicationRecord) is a Ruby class inheriting from Rails base classes.
Modules & Mixins (module Crud)-->Rails relies heavily on modules to add shared functionality across models and controllers (e.g., concern modules).
bcrypt (BCrypt::Password)-->Rails has built-in authentication! Adding has_secure_password to a model uses bcrypt under the hood—the exact logic you just mastered in your scripts.
